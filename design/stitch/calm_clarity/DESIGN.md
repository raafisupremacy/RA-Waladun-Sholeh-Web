## Brand & Style

This design system embraces an aesthetic of absolute clarity, reduction, and quiet authority inspired by high-end typography-first interface design. Built specifically for educational administrative ecosystems serving diverse user roles—from school treasurers and principals to teachers and parents—the interface eliminates visual noise, decorative illustration, and arbitrary ornamentation.

### Personality & Tone
- **Calm & Objective:** Interfaces communicate with precision and understated confidence. Emotional states are modulated through typographic hierarchy and structural order rather than emotional graphics or cheerful mascots.
- **Heroic Data:** Numerical data, verification statuses, and transactional milestones act as the visual heroes of screens.
- **Plain, Polite Bahasa Indonesia:** Microcopy is direct, functional, and devoid of hyperbolic welcomes or exclamation marks.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Minimalism** with **Modern Operating System Craftsmanship**—drawing directly from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. It leverages crisp grouped surfaces, high-fidelity neutral scales, precise hairlines, and translucent structural bars with backdrop filtration.

## Colors

The color system relies on a rigorous neutral ramp built from cold titanium-grays paired with a single, highly deliberate accent blue (`#0071E3`). Color is functional rather than decorative—employed exclusively to indicate interactive affordance, focus states, or transactional status.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The default page canvas rests on `--bg-canvas` (`#F5F5F7`). Cards, tiles, tables, and modal sheets sit as `--surface` (`#FFFFFF`). Contrast is formed naturally through solid surface separation and subtle 1px dividers rather than cast shadows.
- **Text Tiers:** Primary body copy and headings use `#1D1D1F`. Secondary text, metadata, and labels use `#6E6E73`. Inactive placeholders and tertiary labels drop to `#86868B`.
- **Status Capsules:** Status indicators strictly use paired, accessible foreground-background combinations:
  - *Lunas* (Paid / Settled): `#1D7A3C` on `#E4F6EA`
  - *Menunggu Verifikasi* (Pending Verification): `#A15C00` on `#FFF1D6`
  - *Belum Bayar* (Unpaid): `#6E6E73` on `#EEEEF1`
  - *Ditolak* (Rejected): `#C4271B` on `#FDE7E5`
- **Assessment Steps:** The 4-tier kindergarten developmental milestones (BB, MB, BSH, BSB) progress monochromatically through step shades of blue (`#EEF2F8` to `#0071E3`), ensuring typography remains readable over every level.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses the system font stack `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "SF Pro Text", Inter, sans-serif`. For production consistency across platforms, tokens are mapped to `Inter`.

### Composition Rules
- **Discipline of Scale:** Limit screens to a maximum of 4 type sizes to maintain calm, uncluttered layouts.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric displays (prices, currency `Rp`, percentages, student counts, dates) use tabular figure formatting (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`) to ensure perfect vertical alignment across tables and tiles.
- **Alignment:** Titles are left-aligned by default. Centered text is restricted exclusively to single-purpose utility views such as authentication screens.
- **Styling Prohibitions:** No serifs, handwriting scripts, decorative outlines, or simulated letterforms. All-caps is prohibited with the exception of 11-12px status capsules and developmental tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8px spatial grid. Layout containers adapt responsively while preserving strict structural bounding rules:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile:** 0–639px (Baseline designed at 390px)
  - **Tablet:** 640–1023px (Baseline designed at 834px)
  - **Desktop:** 1024px+ (Baseline designed at 1440px)

- **Max Width Bounds:**
  - Standard dashboards and data tables: Centered with a maximum width of `1200px`.
  - Focused forms, reading views, and parental workflows: Constrained to `680px` maximum width. Text lines never stretch across full viewports.

- **Responsive Grid Logic:**
  - **Mobile:** 1-column layouts, tiles span full width, tables reflow into stacked grouped rows.
  - **Tablet:** 2-column bento grids, split master-detail layouts (280px list pane).
  - **Desktop:** 3-to-4 column bento arrangements, 340px list master view, full comprehensive tables with right-aligned numeric metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy in this design system avoids simulated real-world drop shadows or deep z-axis stacking. The layout remains predominantly flat and tactile through clear boundary contrasts:

### 1. Planar Contrast
Surfaces achieve distinction via clean perimeter separation: pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers placed against the neutral grey canvas (`#F5F5F7`), delineated by crisp 1px hairlines (`#E5E5EA`).

### 2. Translucency & Frosted Glass
Structural interface elements—specifically the Global Header, Sticky Local Subnav, Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar, and Sticky Action Footers—use a frosted glass surface:
- Background: `rgba(251, 251, 253, 0.8)`
- Backdrop Blur: `saturate(180%) blur(20px)`
- Border: 1px solid hairline (`#E5E5EA`) at the separating edge.
No decorative glass or glowing panels are permitted elsewhere.

### 3. Floating Modals & Sheets
Only true ephemeral overlays (dropdown menus, bottom sheets, centered modal alerts) receive elevation:
- Drop Shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`
- Edge Definition: 1px hairline border in `#E5E5EA` to maintain edge fidelity across high-resolution displays.

## Shapes

Corner radii establish structural continuity across various hierarchy levels:
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`):** Used universally for primary action buttons, filter tags, interactive action capsules, and status indicators.
- **Tiles & Bento Containers:** `28px` on desktop and tablet; dynamically reduced to `20px` on mobile viewports.
- **Tables, Panels, and Sheets:** Fixed `20px` corner radius. Mobile bottom sheets apply `20px` exclusively to the top-left and top-right vertices.
- **Form Inputs & Secondary Elements:** `12px` radius across input fields, text areas, and contextual segmented control indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button:** Solid `--accent` (`#0071E3`) pill shape, white typography, minimum 44px height (48px on touch/mobile devices). On mobile, primary actions span full container width.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill container filled with `--accent-soft` (`#E8F1FD`) and `--accent` text, or a minimal text link paired with a trailing chevron (`Lihat detail ›`).
- **Destructive Button:** Neutral pill with a 1px border of `#C4271B` and `#C4271B` text.
- **Interaction Feedback:** Active/pressed state triggers a subtle physical scale down to `0.98` with a 150ms transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, solid white fill, 1px `#E5E5EA` border, `12px` border radius. 
- **Focus State:** 1px `#0071E3` border accompanied by an outer halo ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.25)`.
- **Labels:** Positioned strictly above fields, styled in 13px `--text-secondary` (`#6E6E73`). Error copy appears directly beneath in `#C4271B`.
- **Segmented Controls:** Enclosed container in `#EEEEF1` with an active sliding pill thumb in pure `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an subtle ambient hairline border. On small screens with dense criteria, segmented controls support smooth horizontal panning without showing scrollbars.

### Grouped Lists & Tables
- **Grouped Lists (Mobile Default):** Pure white container with `20px` rounded corners. Rows measure a comfortable 56px in height with 1px hairlines inset past leading icons or text labels. Terminal rows feature trailing navigation chevrons.
- **Desktop Tables:** Container with `20px` radius, header rows set to 12-13px uppercase `--text-secondary`, content rows 48-52px tall. Rows highlight subtly on cursor hover (`#FAFAFC`). Numeric metrics are strictly right-aligned with tabular numerals.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- Displayed as fully-rounded pills with vertical padding of 4px and horizontal padding of 10px. Typography uses 12px weight 500. Badges always pair color with plain text describing status—never color alone.

### Modals & Transient Navigation
- **Mobile Bottom Sheets:** Slide up from bottom with 20px top corner radius, backdrop scrim at 40% black, and an accessible top grabber handle (`36px × 5px` pill in `#D1D1D6`).
- **Desktop Dialogs:** Centered fixed-width containers (`480px`) with 20px uniform radius and standard modal shadow.
- **Bottom Tab Bar:** Fixed 4-item mobile footer, 52px height plus safe area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), rendered with 1.5px stroke Lucide icons and 11px label typography.
